# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: HTML, CSS and JavaScript without dependencies, because the portfolio should open locally in a browser and remain simple to maintain.

## Users

Agencies and recruiters reviewing Víctor Ramires's professional experience, audiovisual work and creative direction, with the goal of evaluating fit and making contact.

## Product Purpose

Present Víctor's professional trajectory and selected creative work in a portfolio that makes his audiovisual experience easy to assess and provides direct contact options.

## Positioning

An audiovisual-first portfolio grounded in hands-on work across production, capture, editing and post-production, connected to a broader practice in communication, design and creative direction.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a single-page portfolio on desktop or mobile, explore selected projects and professional experience, and contact Víctor by email or WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static, responsive web page with anchor navigation and embedded or locally available project media.
- Local files should be reused where appropriate without modifying or replacing the originals.
- Missing project media must remain clearly identified placeholders; do not fabricate assets, credits, or links.
- Do not display social-network links or social-profile placeholders; email and WhatsApp are the direct contact channels.
- The site copy is in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Víctor Ramires.
- The requested visual direction uses black and white page surfaces, with restrained purple accents and the supplied Gilroy typeface.
- The main professional focus is audiovisual production, video capture, photography and creative direction, while retaining his communication and design experience.

## Evidence on Hand

- `Doc ComCom 2.2024 v3 (1080p).mp4` is available locally.
- Local Apuama video files are available at the project root.
- Local Diego Zaka and Recanto das Flores photographs are available at the project root, with additional project archives.
- The user supplied a portrait with transparent background and a portrait with its background, plus the Gilroy font family.
- The root contains a one-page résumé PDF and a ten-page design portfolio PDF with selected campaign, branding, and social-content artwork.
- Social-profile links are intentionally excluded from the site.

## Product Principles

- Lead with selected audiovisual work and real project material.
- Make Víctor's role and the context of each project clear without inventing credits.
- Present the multidisciplinary trajectory as connected experience, not as a generic résumé.
- Keep project assets and placeholders honest about what is and is not available.
- Make exploration and contact straightforward on every screen size.
- Present both audiovisual projects and selected design work without inventing credits or project context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide semantic page structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image text, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
